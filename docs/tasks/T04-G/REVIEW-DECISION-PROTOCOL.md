# Human Review Decision Protocol

Review independently where practical. Assess real function/version, novelty against linked baseline and search limits, policy-backed observable oracle, fixture/isolation/reset/cleanup, and Selenium feasibility. Rate SUPPORTED/PARTIAL/UNSUPPORTED/UNKNOWN with evidence; unknown is a blocker.

Decisions: ACCEPTED_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION, REVISE, REJECTED, BLOCKED. Record candidate/version, reviewer identity, timezone timestamp, decision, evidence/provenance, rationale, conditions and disagreement. Two reviewers act independently where practical; disagreement stays pending for Team Lead. Never fabricate approval.
